Help Guide

Answers to common questions about using the Salem City Community Event Portal.

Registration

How do I register for an event?

Go to the "Register" section on the main page. Fill in your name, email, phone number, preferred date, and choose the event type. Click "Confirm Registration" to complete your booking.

Is there a registration fee?

It depends on the event. Cultural fests, health camps, and the Independence Day parade are free. Workshops, sports carnivals, and tech summits carry a nominal fee shown in the event listing and during registration.

Can I register for multiple participants?

Yes. The registration form includes a "Number of Participants" field. You can register up to 10 people per submission.

Location & Events

How does the "Find Nearby Events" feature work?

Click the "Find Nearby Events" button under the Location section. Your browser will request permission to access your GPS location. Once granted, it displays your coordinates and lists the closest upcoming events with approximate distances.

What if I deny location access?

The portal will display a clear error message asking you to enable location permissions in your browser settings. No data is stored if you deny access.

Preferences & Data

What does "Save Preference" do?

The portal saves your preferred event type to your browser's local storage. The next time you visit, your preference is automatically restored, and matching events are highlighted for you.

How do I clear my saved data?

Click the "Clear Preferences" button in the Preferences section. This removes all data saved in both localStorage and sessionStorage for this portal.

Feedback & Support

How do I submit feedback for a past event?

Scroll to the "Event Feedback" section, select the event you attended, write your comments, and click "Submit Feedback". Feedback is reviewed by the city events team.

Why does a warning appear when I try to leave the page?

If you have started filling in the registration form but haven't submitted it yet, the portal will warn you before you navigate away. This prevents accidental loss of your details.
