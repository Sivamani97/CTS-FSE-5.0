Help Guide

Answers to common questions about using the Salem City Community Event Portal.

Registration

How do I register for an event?

Go to the Register section, fill in your name, email, phone, date, and event type, then click Confirm Registration.

Is there a registration fee?

It depends on the event. Cultural fests, health camps, and the parade are free. Workshops and the tech summit carry a nominal fee shown during registration.

Can I register multiple participants?

Yes. The Participants field accepts up to 10 per submission.

Location Feature

How does Find Nearby Events work?

Click the button in the Location section. Your browser will request GPS permission. Once granted, it shows your coordinates and lists the closest events.

What if I deny location access?

The portal displays a clear error. No data is stored if you deny access.

Preferences & Data

What does saving a preference do?

Your preferred event type is saved to your browser's localStorage. It is automatically restored on your next visit.

How do I clear my saved data?

Click Clear Preferences in the Settings section. This removes all localStorage and sessionStorage data for this portal.

Other Questions

Why does a warning appear when I leave?

If you have started filling in the registration form but haven't submitted it, the portal warns you to prevent accidental data loss.

How do I submit feedback?

Go to the Feedback section, choose the event you attended, write your comments, and click Submit Feedback.
