Salem City Bulletin — June 2026

Published by the Salem Municipal Events Office · Volume 12, Issue 6

The Salem City Council is proud to announce a packed calendar of community events for the upcoming season. This summer promises to be one of the most vibrant in recent memory, with cultural programmes, health initiatives, and civic participation drives running from June through August across all major city zones.

The Heritage Cultural Fest, returning to Yercaud Road Ground after a brief hiatus, will feature over forty folk artists from across Tamil Nadu. Organisers expect more than two thousand attendees over the course of the two-day event, making it the single largest community gathering this year.

"Participation in civic events strengthens the social fabric of our neighbourhoods." — Commissioner, Salem Municipal Corporation

The Green Salem Workshop series, now in its fourth year, has drawn consistently strong interest from residents aged eighteen to forty-five. This year's session at Town Hall will include hands-on demonstrations of urban composting and rooftop solar installations, followed by an open Q&A with city engineers.

Youth sports registrations have already crossed the five hundred mark, a new record. The Sports Carnival committee has expanded the chess and athletics tracks to accommodate the surge in interest from school-age participants. Parents are encouraged to register their children early via the online portal.

The Senior Citizens Health Camp continues its tradition of free comprehensive screenings for residents above the age of sixty. This year, ophthalmology and dental checkup stations have been added, sponsored by two city hospitals in partnership with the corporation.

Looking ahead, the Civic Tech Summit in August will bring together developers, urban planners, and government officials to discuss the city's five-year digital infrastructure roadmap. Attendance is open to all residents with a valid registration.
